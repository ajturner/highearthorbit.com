I'm Andrew Turner, CTO of [ArcGIS Hub](https://hub.arcgis.com) and Director of Esri's R&D Center in Washington, DC. I lead product architecture, roadmap and delivery for the team building Hub: open access to authoritative government data, and tools for communities to use it. Organizations from the World Bank and the World Resources Institute to federal agencies and city governments use it as public digital infrastructure for civic engagement.

My work sits where geospatial technology, open data and government meet. Before Esri I was CTO of GeoIQ (GeoCommons), which Esri acquired in 2012, and co-founded Mapufacture, which GeoIQ acquired in 2008. I also co-founded [CrisisCommons](https://crisiscommons.org/), a volunteer community that builds technology for disaster response and recovery. Government Technology named my CrisisCommons co-founders Heather Blanchard, Noel Dickover and me to its [2012 Top 25 Doers, Dreamers and Drivers](https://www.govtech.com/top-25/CrisisCommons-Co-Founders.html).

## Community and standards

I am an elected charter member of the [OSGeo Foundation](https://www.osgeo.org/), a long-time [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) contributor, and was one of the early organizers of WhereCamp. I have worked on open standards through the Open Geospatial Consortium and the W3C, and served on the National Academies' [Geographical Sciences Committee](/articles/national-academies-of-sciences-engineering-and-medicine/).

## Writing

I wrote [*Introduction to Neogeography*](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/introduction-to-neogeography/9780596529956/) (O'Reilly, 2006) and the O'Reilly Radar report *Where 2.0: The State of the Geospatial Web* (2008). My articles have appeared in [Linux Journal](https://www.linuxjournal.com/article/7856), [MacTech](https://www.mactech.com/articles/mactech/Vol.23/23.02/AutomateYourPlace2/index.html) and Make, and on the Esri and GeoIQ blogs. The [Writing](/articles/) page lists them alongside everything published here since 2004.

## Aerospace

I have a BS in aerospace engineering, with a computer science minor, from the University of Virginia, where my thesis was a [semi-autonomous control system for a solar-powered, high-altitude airship](/assets/files/Turner%20-%20Development%20of%20a%20Semi-Autonomous%20Control%20System%20for%20the%20UVA%20Solar%20Airship%20Aztec.pdf). My MS in aerospace engineering from Virginia Tech covered [neural networks for adaptive spacecraft attitude control](/assets/files/Turner%20-%20Adaptive%20Spacecraft%20Attitude%20Control%20Using%20Neural%20Networks.pdf), and I built [Open-SESSAME](https://github.com/ajturner/spacecraft), an [open-source spacecraft simulation framework](/assets/files/Turner%20-%20An%20Open-Source%20Extensible%20Spacecraft%20Simulation%20Framework.pdf). Before mapping, I worked on spacecraft control systems, including the [GOCE](/articles/goce-the-hidden-life-of-a-satellite/) gravity-mapping satellite for Astrium.
