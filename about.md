I'm Andrew Turner, CTO of [ArcGIS Hub](https://hub.arcgis.com) at Esri in Washington, DC. I lead product architecture, roadmap and delivery for the team building Hub: open access to authoritative government data, and tools for communities to use it.

My work sits where geospatial technology, open data and government meet. Before Esri I was CTO of GeoIQ (GeoCommons) and co-founded Mapufacture and [CrisisCommons](https://crisiscommons.org/), a volunteer community that builds technology for disaster response and recovery.

## Writing

I wrote [*Introduction to Neogeography*](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/introduction-to-neogeography/9780596529956/) (O'Reilly, 2006) and the *Where 2.0: The State of the Geospatial Web* report. I've written for Make, Linux Journal and MacTech, and here since 2004.

## Aerospace

I have a BS in aerospace engineering, with a computer science minor, from the University of Virginia, where my thesis was a [semi-autonomous control system for a solar-powered, high-altitude airship](/assets/files/Turner%20-%20Development%20of%20a%20Semi-Autonomous%20Control%20System%20for%20the%20UVA%20Solar%20Airship%20Aztec.pdf). My MS in aerospace engineering from Virginia Tech covered [neural networks for adaptive spacecraft attitude control](/assets/files/Turner%20-%20Adaptive%20Spacecraft%20Attitude%20Control%20Using%20Neural%20Networks.pdf), and I built [Open-SESSAME](https://github.com/ajturner/spacecraft), an [open-source spacecraft simulation framework](/assets/files/Turner%20-%20An%20Open-Source%20Extensible%20Spacecraft%20Simulation%20Framework.pdf).
