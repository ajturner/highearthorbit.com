About

I’m Andrew Turner, CTO of ArcGIS Hub at Esri in Washington, DC. I lead product architecture, roadmap and delivery for the team building Hub: open access to authoritative government data, and tools for communities to use it.

My work sits where geospatial technology, open data and government meet. Before Esri I was CTO of GeoIQ (GeoCommons) and co-founded Mapufacture and CrisisCommons, a volunteer community that builds technology for disaster response and recovery.

Writing

I wrote Introduction to Neogeography (O’Reilly, 2006) and the Where 2.0: The State of the Geospatial Web report. I’ve written for Make, Linux Journal and MacTech, and here since 2004.

Aerospace

I have a BS in aerospace engineering, with a computer science minor, from the University of Virginia, where my thesis was a semi-autonomous control system for a solar-powered, high-altitude airship. My MS in aerospace engineering from Virginia Tech covered neural networks for adaptive spacecraft attitude control, and I built Open-SESSAME, an open-source spacecraft simulation framework.
