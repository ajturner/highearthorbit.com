Every layer comes from a reduced physical model calibrated to published global numbers (the CERES energy budget, GISTEMP warming and the Mauna Loa CO2 record), not from observations, and the page says so on every layer. It is framed as a page in a fictional city's climate resilience initiative, to show how a Hub site could explain a hard physical system. Built with the ArcGIS Maps SDK, Calcite and Lit.
