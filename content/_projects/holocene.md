A pan-and-zoom timeline of human civilization dated in the Holocene Era calendar, where year 1 falls near the first permanent settlements. It has a Milestones view and a World view with one swimlane per region, a movable "meanwhile" line that reads across every region at one year, and surprising comparisons that make a counterintuitive fact visible. A 17 by 11 inch poster shows the whole span with two enlarged sections.
