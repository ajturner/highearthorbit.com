GeoCommons was GeoIQ's public data commons: anyone could upload a dataset, style it into a map, run analysis in the browser and share the result. It carried the first wave of open government data releases, powered crisis mapping for the Haiti earthquake and later responses, and its open-source pieces (Acetate, Intaglio, the geocoder) are listed as separate projects. GeoIQ was acquired by Esri in 2012; GeoCommons closed in 2015 as its ideas moved into ArcGIS Open Data and then ArcGIS Hub.
