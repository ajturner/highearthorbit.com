Forty-two places across DC, Virginia, Maryland and Pennsylvania, each with a short narrative, a numbered source on every statement and a note on what is uncertain. Tabs cover the peoples, nine threads that link places, a timeline from 18,000 years ago to 2021, and a library of books, recordings and places to visit. Markers say how precise each location is: conjectural and sensitive sites, such as burials, are drawn blurred on purpose.
