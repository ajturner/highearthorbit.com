---

In the late 2000s several interactive map libraries appeared at once, each adding features and basemap data quickly while licensing terms kept changing. Developers had to choose one up front, and often needed different libraries across one application. Mapstraction let an application switch providers with one line of configuration, and gave open-source and commercial maps the same API.
