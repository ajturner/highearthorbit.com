The companion to NatureScope. NatureScope answers what is around me; FieldKey trains whether I can recognize it myself. It starts with fifty Mid-Atlantic species and their field marks, schedules review with the FSRS algorithm, and syncs progress through an optional ArcGIS Online account. Built with Lit, Calcite and the ArcGIS Maps SDK.
