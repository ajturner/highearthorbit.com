**Work.** I lead the ArcGIS Hub team at Esri's R&D Center in Washington, DC. This autumn we opened a [developer documentation site](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/developers/sharing-collaboration/developers-lounge-creating-a-documentation-site-to-help-developers-navigate-arcgis-hub) so developers can find Hub's APIs, integrate trusted data and build on Hub, and I wrote about it with the Esri Developer's Lounge.

**Speaking.** In April I was part of [FedGeoDay 2026](/talks/2026-04-22-fedgeoday-2026/) in Washington. Last November at FOSS4G North America I spoke on [building the global spatial data infrastructure](/talks/2025-11-04-building-the-global-spatial-data-infrastructure/) with open standards and community.

**This site.** I am moving more than twenty years of writing, talks, projects and photos into one repository, so that every article since 2004 and every recorded talk is in one place and kept in sync with where it was first published.
